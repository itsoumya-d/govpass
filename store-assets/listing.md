### Description
Navigate complex government services without the confusion. GovPass uses AI to explain your benefits, fill out forms, find local assistance programs, and guide you through government processes step by step.

We have helped 25,000 people access over $180 million in government benefits they did not know they were entitled to. Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, housing assistance, SNAP, veterans benefits, disability programs — the money is there. The problem is navigating the bureaucracy to claim what is yours. GovPass translates government complexity into clear, simple steps — in plain English, at every income level, for every life situation.

**What GovPass Does for You:**

- Run an AI eligibility check across 200+ federal and state benefit programs in under 2 minutes
- Get step-by-step guidance for any government form — SSA, IRS, USCIS, DMV, and more
- Find local assistance programs, food banks, housing aid, and emergency services near you
- Track benefit application deadlines with countdown badges so you never miss an enrollment window
- Receive personalized benefit alerts when a new program matches your profile
- Prepare for government appointments with AI-generated checklists of exactly what to bring
- Store important government IDs and documents in a secure organized vault

**Key Features:**

**AI Benefits Eligibility Scanner**
Answer a few simple questions about your household, income, age, and situation — GovPass instantly cross-checks against 200+ federal and state programs and shows exactly which benefits you likely qualify for, how much you could receive, and how to apply. No jargon. No confusion.

**Benefit Deadline Countdown Badges**
Many programs have strict application windows — Medicare enrollment periods, SNAP renewal deadlines, housing assistance lotteries. GovPass shows animated countdown badges on every time-sensitive benefit so you never miss a filing deadline. Missing a window can mean waiting 12 more months.

**Form Fill Guidance**
Government forms are notoriously confusing. GovPass walks you through any federal or state form field by field, explains what each question is really asking, flags common mistakes, and helps you attach the right supporting documents. The OCR step indicator tracks your progress through each section.

**Local Assistance Finder**
Beyond federal programs, GovPass locates community resources near you — food banks, rental assistance, utility subsidies, free legal aid, childcare subsidies, and emergency services. Updated weekly with verified locations and hours.

**Government Appointment Prep**
Before any government appointment — Social Security office, DMV, immigration interview — GovPass generates a personalized prep checklist with exactly what to bring, what questions to expect, and how to present your information.

**Secure Document Vault**
Store digital copies of your Social Security card, birth certificate, ID, insurance cards, and tax documents. Organized, encrypted, and accessible whenever government services ask for them.

**Program Coverage Includes:**
SNAP, Medicaid, CHIP, Medicare Savings Programs, LIHEAP Energy Assistance, Section 8 Housing, WIC, SSDI, SSI, TANF, Veterans Benefits (VA), Earned Income Tax Credit, Child Tax Credit, Unemployment Insurance, and 185+ more programs.

**B2B: Nonprofit and Senior Center Licensing**
GovPass offers per-seat licensing for nonprofits, senior centers, community health workers, and government contractors who help clients navigate benefits. Contact us for nonprofit and B2B pricing.

**Who Uses GovPass:**
- Seniors navigating Social Security, Medicare, and prescription drug coverage
- Low-income families finding SNAP, housing assistance, and childcare subsidies
- Veterans seeking VA benefits, disability ratings, and healthcare enrollment
- Immigrants navigating USCIS, work authorization, and state benefit eligibility
- Caregivers helping elderly or disabled family members access what they need
