# GovPass — App Store Listing

## App Name
GovPass: Benefits Finder

## Subtitle (30 chars max)
SNAP, Medicaid & Gov Benefits

## Description (4000 chars max)
Thousands of dollars in benefits may already be waiting for you. Find out in minutes — for free.

GovPass helps you discover every government benefit program you qualify for — SNAP, Medicaid, housing assistance, utility relief, and more — then walks you through applying step by step.

Billions of dollars in government benefits go unclaimed every year — not because people don't qualify, but because the system is complicated, fragmented, and hard to navigate. GovPass changes that. Answer a few straightforward questions and get a personalized list of programs you're eligible for, right now.

### Key Features:

**Eligibility Engine**
Our AI-powered questionnaire asks about your household size, income, employment status, age, disability status, citizenship, and more — then instantly checks your eligibility against 15+ federal and state benefit programs using current Federal Poverty Level guidelines.

**Program Coverage Includes:**
- SNAP (Food Stamps)
- Medicaid & CHIP
- Medicare Savings Programs
- LIHEAP (Energy Assistance)
- Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher
- WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)
- Social Security Disability (SSDI)
- Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
- TANF (Temporary Assistance)
- Head Start & Early Childhood Programs
- Unemployment Insurance
- Veterans Benefits (VA)
- Free/Reduced School Lunch
- Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)
- Child Tax Credit

**Step-by-Step Application Guidance**
For each program you qualify for, GovPass provides plain-language instructions, required documents checklist, where to apply (online portal, local office, or phone), and estimated processing time.

**Document Checklist**
Know exactly what to gather before you start. GovPass gives you a personalized document checklist — birth certificates, pay stubs, lease agreements, utility bills — so your application is complete the first time.

**Status Tracking**
Log your applications and track their status. Set reminders for renewal deadlines so you never lose benefits due to a missed recertification.

**Local Office Finder**
Find the nearest SNAP office, Medicaid office, or benefits center with directions and hours. Know exactly where to go and when they're open before you make the trip.

**Free to Use**
GovPass is completely free. We believe everyone who qualifies for help should be able to get it — without paying for the privilege of navigating a complex system.

### Who Uses GovPass:
- Families experiencing a sudden job loss or income reduction
- Seniors on fixed incomes exploring Medicare Savings Programs
- Parents of young children checking WIC and CHIP eligibility
- Veterans navigating VA benefits for the first time
- Social workers and case managers helping clients identify available resources

### Why GovPass:
The average eligible family leaves over $5,000 per year in unclaimed benefits on the table. GovPass is built to close that gap — one household at a time. No bureaucratic jargon, no endless PDF forms, no confusion. Just clear answers about what you qualify for and how to get it.

You deserve the help that's already waiting for you.
